CardVault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

The short version: CardVault stores everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. We never receive, see, or transmit your contacts, card images, or any other information you put into the app.

Who we are

CardVault is developed by Oscar Ibarra. This policy explains how the CardVault mobile application handles information. It applies to the app only.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. CardVault has no user accounts, no sign-in, no back-end server, and no analytics or tracking software. No information leaves your device as a result of ordinary use of the app.

Information stored on your device

CardVault saves the data you create directly on your device, in a local database that only the app can read. That data may include:

  • Contact details captured from business cards or entered by hand — names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, and similar fields
  • Photographs of the business cards you scan, including front and back images
  • Services, tags, and notes you add to a contact
  • Your app settings and preferences

This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Because it is stored locally and not on a server, we cannot recover it for you if it is lost, so we recommend using the backup export in Settings.

Camera and photos

CardVault asks for camera access so you can photograph business cards. Images captured this way are saved to the app's own storage on your device. The app does not upload them anywhere. If you grant access to your photo library, it is used only to let you select an existing image to import.

Text recognition

When you scan a card, CardVault reads the text on it using on-device text recognition provided by Google's ML Kit. This processing happens entirely on your device. The card image is not sent to Google, to us, or to any other server in order to be read.

NFC

If you use the tap-to-read feature, CardVault reads contact information from an NFC tag you hold your device against. That information is handled the same way as any other contact in the app: stored locally, and not transmitted.

Sharing and export

CardVault can share a contact as a vCard and can export a full backup of your data. These actions happen only when you start them, and the data goes wherever you choose to send it — email, messages, a file in your storage, or another app. Once you share or export data, this policy no longer governs it; the receiving service's own terms apply.

Purchases

CardVault offers a one-time purchase that unlocks the full app. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple confirms to the app whether the purchase was made; nothing else about you is shared with us. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

CardVault includes no advertising, no analytics services, and no social media integrations. The only third parties involved are Apple, which processes purchases and distributes the app, and Google's ML Kit, which provides the on-device text recognition described above.

Children

CardVault is a business tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly. You can edit or delete any contact in the app, revoke camera or photo permissions in your device settings at any time, export a backup of everything, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
